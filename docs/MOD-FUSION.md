# Weapon mods

Open the crafting grid and combine two pieces of paper, a copper ingot and redstone dust to make an **Arsenal Mod Pack**. Put the pieces of paper in separate slots. Hold the pack in either hand and right-click. The card appears on screen and enters your character’s collection at rank 0. Each pack picks uniformly from the cards you still need. A pack stays sealed when you own everything in its pool.

| To focus an unopened Arsenal Mod Pack | Add | Possible unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Iron ingot | 11 rifle, shotgun and shared primary cards |
| Secondary | Copper ingot | 5 sidearm cards |
| Melee | Flint | 6 melee cards |

Visit either Gunsmith and open **Warframe → Weapon Mods**. Select the specific weapon you want to change, then select a card and choose **Install mod**. Choose **Rank up** to spend Minecraft XP levels on the card. The next rank number is its price. Ranks 1 through 5 cost 15 levels altogether. The card already grants its first bonus at rank 0. Installation and removal are free. Every distinct compatible card fits on the same weapon.

One unlocked Serration can serve a Braton and a Soma at once. Ranking it improves both. A second Braton can have an entirely different selection. If you hand the first rifle to a friend, the installed choices travel with it. Your friend gets their own ranks. A card they have yet to unlock appears as **Dormant**. The Foundry protects weapons with cards installed when charging a base weapon for an upgraded variant.
