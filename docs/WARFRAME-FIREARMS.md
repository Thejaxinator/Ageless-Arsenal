# Warframe firearms

Choose **Warframe → Blueprints** at either Gunsmith to build the 27 ranged weapons in this table. Their trigger, magazine, firing modes and presentation are separate from the Destiny weapon pool. A weapon uses one inventory slot, including paired sidearms. The models, alternate-fire effects and game-balance values are Minecraft adaptations. Armor, shields, friendly-fire settings and real block cover participate in combat.

## Playing

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Attack | Fire. Hold for automatic weapons, including Ignis and Nukor. Tap once for a pistol shot or an entire rifle burst. |
| Use item | Aim. Perigale has stronger magnification. Vesper marks visible weak points in its sight. |
| Reload (default R) | Reload from the weapon's unlimited reserve. Cantare recalls its blades at the start of a reload. The Bubonico family automatically regenerates its charge. |
| Warframe alternate fire (default G) | Launch a Cedo glaive or start a Bubonico three-shot viral salvo. Rebind it in the ordinary Minecraft controls menu. |
| Sneak and use on a block | Interact with a workstation while carrying a ranged weapon. |

The left HUD shows the live magazine count and its capacity. Tenno weapons use their own ammo display alongside the existing ability HUD and honor its theme, placement and scale. The actual item stores its magazine across holstering, dropping, reloading the world and reconnecting. Unfinished bursts cancel on weapon changes. The two pistols occupy one hotbar slot. Their two first-person muzzles and opposite third-person hand follow the player. An equipped vanilla offhand takes precedence over the secondary third-person model.

## Traits in Minecraft

- **AX-52.** A hip-fired sequence spends one round per four shots. Aimed upper-body weak points double the shot's damage.
- **Grakata, Braton and Burston families.** Grakatas fire quickly with substantial hip spread. Bratons trade that cadence for stronger shots. Burstons release three rounds two ticks apart. Both Twin Grakatas muzzles fire during the same cycle and consume independent rounds.
- **Ignis.** A widening heat stream can hit multiple visible targets and ignites them for two seconds. Wraith extends the reach.
- **Brakk.** Ten pellets share each round's damage. Falloff starts beyond seven blocks. Kuva brings a larger magazine and stronger pattern.
- **Nukor.** Microwave hits apply Weakness. The Kuva model chains from the first target to two more within five blocks of each preceding target, at 65% and 42% damage. Each link requires visibility.
- **Cedo.** The normal trigger fires six pellets. Each distinct active effect and burning condition adds 18% damage, up to five bonuses. The alternate glaive costs no rounds, seeks up to five visible targets, and cycles between cold, heat, toxin and weakness. A fresh disc becomes available in 36 ticks.
- **Vesper 77.** The suppressed report is quieter than a rifle shot. Aiming marks unobstructed upper weak points with amber pips. An aimed weak-point shot doubles damage.
- **Perigale.** Each trigger launches a four-round burst. Four upper-body weak points within one burst, or a weak-point kill, grant Gale Force for 80 ticks. Shots during that window consume no rounds.
- **Soma.** Successive shots spool from six to two ticks apart. More than eight idle ticks reset the spool. Weak-point hits deal 1.9× damage.
- **Cantare.** Blades lodge in the environment or at a target’s impact position for up to 18 seconds. Reloading calls back that particular weapon's blades. An enemy crossed on the way back takes 2.1× base damage per blade. Solid cover intercepts the return.
- **Torid.** Its arcing spore grenade bursts on impact. A toxin field persists for four seconds and checks exposed victims twice a second.
- **Aksondol.** The two pistols alternate. Cold hits slow a target. Completing a reload drops a three-second freezing field with radius in blocks of 1.2 + 0.35 × spent magazine rounds.
- **Bubonico.** Primary fire projects seven toxic pellets. Alternate fire releases up to three arcing viral blasts, each costing three charge. A battery begins regenerating 30 ticks after firing, or two ticks after running completely dry. The standard version regenerates one charge every three ticks, Coda every two. The Coda model has a wider blast and longer weakening effect.

## Engineering

- `com.jax.destiny2mc.tenno.TennoGunSpec` is the ranged catalog. Its family selects the trigger program; each entry supplies magazine, damage, range, reload and cadence. `TennoGunRegistration` registers the items under `destiny2mc:<weapon_id>`.
- `TennoGunItem` is a normal Minecraft item with independent stack state. The stack uses the custom-data keys `wf_mag`, `wf_reload`, `wf_sequence`, `wf_charge_at`, `wf_gale`, and a per-weapon `wf_identity` for projectiles. Existing Destiny perks do not run against these stacks.
- `TennoIntent` sends an action and an aiming flag. The server obtains the actual main-hand stack and computes origin, direction, ray limits, projectile counts, ammo consumption, targets and all damage. `TennoBallistics` owns trigger scheduling and reload completion, `TennoHits` owns hit resolution, and `TennoFlights` owns swept projectiles and expiring fields.
- Independent `destiny2mc:tenno_round` damage is tagged as a projectile and bypasses the standard hit cooldown for fast weapons. Armor, shields, alliance checks, pet ownership and server PvP rules still participate. Each shotgun round groups pellets before passing one damage event per target through native defenses.
- First-person rendering uses the `body`, `action` and `load` model parts in `models/armory/<weapon_id>/`. The sight axis is in baked model units. Client reload and shot animation drives only the visuals. `TennoTwinLayer` attaches the additional third-person pistol to the opposite arm independently of the inventory stack.
- `TennoRangeCheck.java` is an isolated NeoForge fixture. It fires the 27 primary attacks at ordinary zombie hitboxes and exercises the distinct burst, reload, chain, alternate-fire, cold-field, blade-return, weak-point and cover behaviors.
