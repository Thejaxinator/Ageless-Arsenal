# Dread visual references

Gameplay photographs and Destiny concept art hosted on Destinypedia guided these Minecraft adaptations. The download contains original cube geometry and generated material textures.

## Grim

The serrated pale membrane spans two jointed spars per side. The thin hind legs, mane of radial spines, split mouth and small injector hang below it.

## Husk

The folded violet hood flares sideways above the golden inner creature. Short paired wrist blades extend from the hands. The polished belly has its own light ribs.

## Geist

The parasite at upper left in the concept sheet informed its kinked dark jaws, small gold core and radiating spines. The model keeps the existing small flying hitbox.

## Attendant

The rectangular crown and round visor belong to a transformed Psion. The ranks share the forked gun. This model uses blue cloth and a restrained version of the enhanced rank’s sharp Stasis growths.
