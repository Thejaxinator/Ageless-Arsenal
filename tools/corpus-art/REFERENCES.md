# Corpus Visual References (Warframe Wiki)

All three Corpus voxel models in `tools/corpus-art/sculpt.py` were authored from official Warframe Wiki renders and unit entries so silhouettes, proportions, armor palettes, and glowing emissive accents match the source faction.

## Faction Overview
- **Corpus** — https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Corpus
  - Industrial merchant cult; sharp geometric silhouettes, machined slate/teal plating, blue-cyan energy rails, and regenerating shield synergy.

## Unit References
1. **Corpus Crewman (`corpus_crewman`, kind `143`)**
   - Wiki page: https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Crewman
   - Reference render: `CrewmanNormal.png` (`https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/warframe/images/2/25/CrewmanNormal.png`)
   - Key visual anchors: tan/brown ribbed industrial jumpsuit, dark slate chest harness with twin breather hoses, rounded black shoulder pauldrons, bulky grey gauntlets, iconic tall tapered boxy grey helmet with recessed glowing cyan visor slit and top sensor fin, carrying a twin-prong **Dera** plasma rifle with a central cyan energy channel.
2. **Corpus MOA (`corpus_moa`, kind `144`)**
   - Wiki page: https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/MOA
   - Reference render: `MOADE.png` (`https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/warframe/images/1/1e/MOADE.png`)
   - Key visual anchors: tall armless bipedal reverse-jointed (digitigrade) walker, sage/teal-green armored thighs and shins with exposed mechanical hydraulic pistons, three-toed mechanical claws (two forward, one rear stabilizer), compact dark-grey forward sensor chassis with cyan optic, and a boxy gold/brass-and-black dorsal plasma cannon.
3. **Corpus Shield Osprey (`corpus_shield_osprey`, kind `145`)**
   - Wiki page: https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Shield_Osprey
   - Reference render: `ShieldOspreyDE.png` (`https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/warframe/images/e/e4/ShieldOspreyDE.png`)
   - Key visual anchors: compact hovering teal central hood/carapace, wide swept teal wing shrouds with raised oval thruster pods and glowing cyan intake louver vents, thin articulated dark-grey underside wing struts, ventral cyan shield-projector dish, and iconic hanging dark-gunmetal twin-prong emitter tail nacelle.
